    <!-- Challenge Section -->
    <section>
      <h2 class="font-heading text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-4 flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-accent text-lg font-mono">01.</span> Challenge
      </h2>
      <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616] space-y-4">
        <p>
          As an active digital agency in the UAE, Digitz Technologies manages concurrent campaigns across diverse industries with varied tech stacks. Key operational demands included:
        </p>
        <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300">
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span><strong>Cross-Account Campaign Scaling:</strong> Managing rapid deployment of Google Ads and Meta campaigns without sacrificing quality control or negative keyword discipline.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span><strong>Attribution Discrepancies:</strong> Resolving discrepancies between Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel data, and client CRM lead recordings.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span><strong>Reporting Bottlenecks:</strong> Manual spreadsheet reporting consumed valuable strategic bandwidth that should have been dedicated to campaign optimization.</span>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Approach Section -->
    <section>
      <h2 class="font-heading text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-4 flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-accent text-lg font-mono">02.</span> Approach
      </h2>
      <div class="space-y-4">
        <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616]">
          <h3 class="font-bold text-white text-base mb-2">Standardized Event Taxonomy</h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300">
            Formulated and implemented a unified Google Tag Manager container architecture for client deployments, establishing consistent event names (`generate_lead`, `click_to_call`, `view_spec_sheet`) across GA4 and ad platforms.
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616]">
          <h3 class="font-bold text-white text-base mb-2">Automated Looker Studio Executive Dashboards</h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300">
            Connected GA4, Google Search Console, and Google Ads APIs directly into customized, real-time client performance dashboards, surfacing live spend, impression share, and cost per acquisition.
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616]">
          <h3 class="font-bold text-white text-base mb-2">Continuous Technical SEO Audits</h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300">
            Instituted weekly crawler health checks for agency client domains to detect canonical conflicts, broken redirects, and schema markup validation issues prior to search indexing impacts.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>
