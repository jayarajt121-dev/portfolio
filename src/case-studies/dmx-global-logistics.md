    <h1 class="font-heading text-3xl md:text-5xl font-bold text-white mb-4">
      DMX Global Logistics: International Freight Search & Logistics Lead Strategy
    </h1>
    <p class="text-lg md:text-xl text-gray-300 font-body leading-relaxed">
      Optimizing search visibility for sea freight, air cargo, and cross-border customs clearance across GCC trade routes.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="space-y-12 text-gray-300 leading-relaxed font-body">
    <!-- Challenge Section -->
    <section>
      <h2 class="font-heading text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-4 flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-accent text-lg font-mono">01.</span> Challenge
      </h2>
      <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616] space-y-4">
        <p>
          International logistics and freight forwarding is an intensely competitive industry dominated by global multi-nationals. Key hurdles faced by DMX Global Logistics included:
        </p>
        <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300">
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span><strong>Generic vs. Commercial Freight Keywords:</strong> Broad searches like "cargo Dubai" generated personal courier package tracking requests rather than commercial container freight contracts.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span><strong>Complex Route Intent:</strong> Cargo buyers search by specific origin-destination corridors (e.g., "Dubai to Rotterdam sea freight FCL") requiring granular landing page alignment.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span><strong>Long B2B Consideration Cycles:</strong> Shippers evaluate freight reliability, customs brokerage credentials, and cargo insurance before requesting rate quotes.</span>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Approach Section -->
    <section>
      <h2 class="font-heading text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-4 flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-accent text-lg font-mono">02.</span> Approach
      </h2>
      <div class="space-y-4">
        <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616]">
          <h3 class="font-bold text-white text-base mb-2">Trade Corridor Keyword Architecture</h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300">
            Conducted deep keyword intent mapping separating LCL (Less than Container Load), FCL (Full Container Load), air charter, and project cargo across high-volume regional trade lanes.
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616]">
          <h3 class="font-bold text-white text-base mb-2">Route-Specific Landing Page Structure</h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300">
            Created streamlined, content-rich landing pages for primary logistics services highlighting transit times, port coverage, customs documentation support, and an instant rate-inquiry modal.
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616]">
          <h3 class="font-bold text-white text-base mb-2">Targeted Google Search Campaigns</h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300">
            Configured commercial search ad campaigns utilizing exact and phrase match for high-margin freight terminology, with extensive negative matching for retail courier and parcel tracking keywords.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Results Section -->
    <section>
      <h2 class="font-heading text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-4 flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-accent text-lg font-mono">03.</span> Results
      </h2>
      <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616] space-y-4">
        <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300">
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span>Eliminated non-commercial consumer parcel traffic from paid search queries, directing 100% of budget toward enterprise shipping inquiries.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span>Secured top search impressions for core commercial freight forwarding and customs clearance queries in the UAE and Middle East.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span>Streamlined rate quotation submissions with qualified cargo details (volume, weight, port of entry).</span>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </section>
