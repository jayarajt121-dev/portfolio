  <div class="space-y-12 text-gray-300 leading-relaxed font-body">
    <!-- Challenge Section -->
    <section>
      <h2 class="font-heading text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-4 flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-accent text-lg font-mono">01.</span> Challenge
      </h2>
      <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616] space-y-4">
        <p>
          Starline Security LLC specializes in large-scale commercial installations, including structured cabling, enterprise CCTV, and biometric access control. Key challenges included:
        </p>
        <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300">
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span><strong>Low Inquiry-to-Opportunity Ratio:</strong> Ads frequently attracted homeowners looking for single-camera doorbells rather than corporate clients requiring 30+ camera systems.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span><strong>High Bounce Rates on Generic Homepages:</strong> Traffic was routed to a dense generic homepage with no immediate commercial value proposition or clear call to action.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span><strong>Tracking Opacity:</strong> No call-tracking or form attribution in place to determine which keywords drove actual site inspection requests.</span>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Approach Section -->
    <section>
      <h2 class="font-heading text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-4 flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-accent text-lg font-mono">02.</span> Approach
      </h2>
      <div class="space-y-4">
        <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616]">
          <h3 class="font-bold text-white text-base mb-2">Intent-Segmented Landing Pages</h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300">
            Designed and built dedicated, high-speed landing pages focused squarely on commercial security requirements (warehouse security, corporate office access control, SIRA compliance standards).
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616]">
          <h3 class="font-bold text-white text-base mb-2">Exact & Phrase Commercial Matching</h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300">
            Rebuilt Google Ads campaigns targeting commercial security keywords with strict exact and phrase match types, eliminating residential terms with over 200 tailored negative keywords.
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616]">
          <h3 class="font-bold text-white text-base mb-2">Event-Level Attribution Setup</h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-gray-300">
            Implemented GA4 and Google Tag Manager triggers for form completions, click-to-call events, and corporate quotation requests, feeding verified conversion signals back into Google Ads bidding algorithms.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Results Section -->
    <section>
      <h2 class="font-heading text-2xl font-bold text-white mb-4 flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-accent text-lg font-mono">03.</span> Results
      </h2>
      <div class="p-6 rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#161616] space-y-4">
        <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300">
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span>Shifted inquiry composition from predominantly residential inquiries to enterprise warehouse and commercial facility projects.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span>Dramatically reduced landing page bounce rates by directing visitors to service-specific landing pages with clear technical specifications.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-2">
            <span class="text-accent mt-0.5">&bull;</span>
            <span>Established full closed-loop visibility on which campaigns and keywords generated commercial site survey requests.</span>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </section>
