Professional Profile

About Jayaraj T

A digital marketer with a computer science foundation, focused on technical SEO, performance marketing, and data-driven client acquisition.

The Engineering Foundation

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Calicut. While traditional marketing often operates on surface intuition, my computer science background provides an engineering foundation: I understand DOM manipulation, rendering lifecycles, structured data parsing, and algorithmic behavior.

Career Journey & Early Internship

My digital marketing path started with an intensive internship at Softroniics in Calicut. There, I was immersed in the mechanics of commercial keyword research, on-page optimization, search engine audit methodology, and ad-copy experimentation. That experience crystallized my approach: marketing is an applied science where data precision directly impacts revenue.

Current Role: Digitz Technologies (UAE)

Currently, I drive digital marketing and search campaigns for clients at Digitz Technologies LLC, a premier UAE-based digital agency. In this role, I manage full-funnel digital marketing across key commercial verticals, including e-commerce, commercial safety systems, enterprise security, and international logistics. My daily responsibilities center on structuring Google Ads search campaigns, setting up end-to-end attribution (GA4, GTM, Meta Pixel), and executing technical SEO overhauls.

Personal Statement: “Somehow, This Is My Profession.”

Many people enter digital marketing by accident; I embraced it deliberately because it is where technical precision meets real-world human intent. Every keyword search is a live query into what a consumer or business genuinely needs at that second.

For freelance clients, I offer a clear, transparent engagement model: no vanity metrics, no fluff reports. I audit your digital footprint, identify where potential customers or ad dollars are being lost, and execute high-precision organic and paid campaigns.
